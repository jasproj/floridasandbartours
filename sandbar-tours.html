What is a sandbar trip?

A sandbar trip is a boat ride to a shallow bank of sand, usually a few hundred yards offshore or inside a pass, where the captain anchors in knee-to-waist-deep water and everyone gets out. You stand, wade, swim, shell and eat off the boat for two to four hours, then ride back. The sandbars on this page are all reached by boat only; most have no shade, no toilets and no vendors, which is the point.

Florida has two kinds. The Gulf Coast sandbars (Shell Key, Anclote Key, Egmont Key, Caladesi, Three Rooker, Keewaydin) are barrier-island shoals with shelling and calm water, reached from St. Pete, Dunedin, Tarpon Springs, Clearwater and Naples. The Panhandle and South Florida sandbars (Crab Island at Destin, Haulover near Miami, the Key West sandbars) are party anchorages where dozens of boats raft up on weekends.

Sandbar Tour FAQs

❯ What is a sandbar trip?

A boat trip to a shallow sand shoal where the captain anchors in knee-to-waist-deep water and you get out to wade, swim and shell. Most run two to four hours and include a stop or two; the sandbar itself has no facilities, so food, drink and shade come on the boat.

❯ How much does a sandbar tour cost in Florida?

Group sandbar tours on this site run $45 to $130 a person: about $45 for the Egmont Key ferry, $55 for a Crab Island pontoon or a Shell Key sunset cruise, $70 to $80 for clear-kayak and shelling trips, and around $130 for a Key West sandbar-and-dolphin combo. Private boats are $400 to $800 for up to six people for three to four hours. Prices are the operators' own on FareHarbor, checked October 2026.

❯ Can I do a dolphin and sandbar tour together?

Yes. Several operators combine them: Key West's Sandbar and Dolphin Adventure, the Shell Key Sandbar Sunset Cruise out of Gulfport, Clearwater dolphin tours with a sandbar stop, and Sarasota's Dolphins, Drinks and Sandbar trip. Dolphins are usually spotted on the ride out through the pass, then the boat anchors at the sandbar.

❯ What should I bring on a sandbar tour?

Bring sunscreen (SPF 30+), a hat, sunglasses, a light cover-up, and a change of clothes. Most tours provide life jackets, but consider bringing waterproof bags for personal items. Check with your tour operator for specific recommendations.

❯ How long is a typical sandbar tour?

Most sandbar tours range from 2 to 4 hours, though full-day excursions (6+ hours) are also available. The exact duration depends on the tour operator and the specific location of the sandbars being visited.

❯ Are sandbar tours suitable for children and families?

Yes! Sandbar tours are family-friendly. The shallow, calm waters are perfect for children. Many tours cater specifically to families and provide flotation devices for younger children. Always check age and swimming ability requirements with your operator.

❯ What's the best time to visit Florida sandbars?

The best time is November through April when weather is mild and waters are calmer. However, sandbars are accessible year-round. Summer offers warmer water but higher humidity and afternoon thunderstorms. Always check weather conditions before your tour.
