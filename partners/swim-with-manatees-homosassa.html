The operation

Twenty-six years on the Homosassa

The company describes itself as a twenty-six-year-old, locally owned operation working the Homosassa River and the Crystal River National Wildlife Refuge. Boats cap at six guests whether you book a seat or the whole thing, which is the number that decides whether a manatee encounter feels like wildlife watching or a queue.

Everything is included: wetsuits, masks and snorkels, in-water guidance, and the captains who hand you a phone and shoot the photos themselves if you have not hired the event photographer. The same boats run Monkey Island river cruises past the island’s resident spider monkeys and a Gulf dolphin quest.

    The site does not list a full roster; reviewers name Captains Cephas, Yves, Sean and Chris, photographer Brooke, and Parker.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Departure

    Dock B, 5297 S Cherokee Way, Homosassa. Boats board early; check-in times run from before sunrise in winter.

    Season

    Winter is peak, when manatees crowd the warm springs. A resident group stays year-round.

    Gear

    Wetsuit, mask and snorkel included. The water sits around 72 degrees, which feels cold in January air.

    Photos

    Captains shoot on your phone; a dedicated event photographer can be added for $124.99.
