The operation

From the Jolly Mon to a fleet

Owners Jim and Cindy Bielinski established the company in 2011, per the About page, with the Jolly Mon, a 27-foot American Mariner sailboat still in service for sails and snorkel trips. The fleet now includes Jolly’Ventures (26-foot tritoon, six guests), Jolly Roger (24-foot tritoon, six) and Jolly Dolphin, a 42-foot tritoon passenger vessel for 28. The site says a new 49-passenger dolphin cruise catamaran is being built.

Trips run in the protected water of Santa Rosa Sound and Pensacola Bay. Reviewers name Captains Carson, Roger and Jim, and crew Mary, Kurt and Cindy. The Blue Angels cruises time the dolphin run to the squadron’s practice days over the beach.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

655 Pensacola Beach Blvd, Slip 20, Pensacola Beach.

Cancellation

24 hours notice; weather calls are the captain’s, with a reschedule or refund if the trip is cancelled.

Drinks

BYOB for guests 21 and up, cans rather than glass; coolers welcome. Bottled water is complimentary.

Blue Angels

Practice cruises run only on the squadron’s published practice days; check the calendar.
