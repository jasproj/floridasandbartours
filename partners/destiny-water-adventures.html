The operation

A Key West parasail captain who moved west

The About page says Captain Kevin O’Neil ran parasail boats in Key West before the family holidayed in this stretch of the Panhandle and decided to stay. The first mates listed are Tammy, October and Ripley, all family. The dock is at 322 Miracle Strip Parkway SW in Fort Walton Beach, which puts Crab Island and the Paradise Islands both inside a short run.

The fleet covers pontoons, jet skis, kayaks and paddleboards, which is the useful spread for a family where not everyone wants the same afternoon. Guided jet ski adventures go out with a lead rider; pontoon rentals go out bareboat, with the Florida boater education card handled through a course the company links from its own site.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

322 Miracle Strip Pkwy SW, Fort Walton Beach, with Crab Island and the Paradise Islands both a short run.

Licence

Florida requires a boater education card for anyone born after January 1, 1988. The company links a course from its site.

Guided vs bareboat

Guided jet ski adventures run with a lead rider; pontoons go out bareboat unless you book the captained charter.

Crab Island

Shallow and busy. A captain is worth it on summer weekends.
