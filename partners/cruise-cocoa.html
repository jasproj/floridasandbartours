The operation

Three captains, two docks, one lagoon

The crew page lists Captain Kevin, Captain Jen and Captain Ritche, each holding a Master 100-ton licence with sailing and towing endorsements. Kevin is described as a lifelong sailor who has run everything from northern cold water to the Caribbean; Jen as a wildlife enthusiast; Ritche as a Midwesterner who left corporate life for the water. The company describes itself as a team of captains, local guides and water lovers rather than a single-owner operation.

Tours leave from Cape Crossing Marina at 290 Marine Harbor Drive, Merritt Island, and from 924 Thomas Barbour Drive in Melbourne. The Melbourne trips run on a vessel the listings call This Island; the company also books a sailing experience under the Knotty Dreams name.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

Cape Crossing Marina, 290 Marine Harbor Drive, Merritt Island, or 924 Thomas Barbour Drive, Melbourne. Check which one your booking uses.

Bioluminescence

Sunset and bioluminescence tours run out of Cocoa Beach in season; dates post on the calendar.

Sightings

Dolphins are wild. The company says they are frequent in the lagoon but does not guarantee them.

Groups

Private charters run $350 to $1,100 depending on boat and time of day.
