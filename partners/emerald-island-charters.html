The operation

Seven boats, one destination

The fleet is built around Crab Island, the shallow submerged sandbar just inside Destin’s East Pass where the water sits waist deep and half the town anchors on a summer afternoon. Seven boats cover the range: a six-passenger slide pontoon and a six-passenger clear boat, ten-passenger single-deck and double-slide toons, a twelve-passenger slide toon with a restroom, an eighteen-passenger luxury pontoon with an inflatable lounge set, and an eighteen-passenger tiki pontoon.

What comes aboard is the same on every boat: Bluetooth speakers, a cooler, goggles, floats and inflatable toys, an eighteen-foot lily pad, paddleboards and a professional captain. The main dock is 530 Harbor Boulevard in Destin; some boats leave from 38 Miracle Strip Parkway SW in Fort Walton Beach.

    The site does not list captains by name; reviewers mention Aiden, Sailor, Diego, Easton, Corbin, Nick and Kayla.

    The one to book

    Double Slide Pontoon with Restroom

    The twelve-passenger boat with two slides and a restroom is the sweet spot for a family day: enough room to spread out, the lily pad and paddleboards aboard, and nobody having to head back early.

    Four hours runs $550 or $600 depending on boat. The smaller double-slide pontoon is $500 and the single-deck six-guest boat $400.
