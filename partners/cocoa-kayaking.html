The operation

Three launch points, guides trained for rescue

The company runs from three launches on the Space Coast: the Thousand Islands at Cocoa Beach, Kiwanis Island Park on Merritt Island and the wildlife refuge at Titusville. Guides are described on the About page as professionally trained and certified in first aid, CPR, AED and kayak rescue. Tours need a minimum of two paddlers to run.

Boats are Wilderness Systems singles rated to 250 pounds, tandems to 425 and Tava stand-up paddleboards to 250, with clear-bottom and pedal kayaks on some tours. The company also runs clean-up paddles at a reduced rate, where guests collect trash from the mangroves as they go.

    The site does not list guides by name; reviewers mention James, Brooke, Andrew, Ian, Savana and Thalia.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Launches

    Thousand Islands at Cocoa Beach, Kiwanis Island Park on Merritt Island, and the wildlife refuge at Titusville. Check which one your tour uses.

    Cancellation

    48 hours for a full refund on parties of 15 or fewer; credit up to four hours before. Groups of 16 or more are non-refundable inside two weeks.

    Weather

    Tours paddle rain or shine when conditions are safe.

    Minimum

    Two paddlers are needed for a tour to run.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Where do tours launch?

    Cocoa Beach, Merritt Island or Titusville, depending on the tour.

    What does it cost?

    $75 per paddler for the mangrove and wildlife tours, $100 for the bioluminescence tours. Checked October 2, 2026.

    Do I need experience?

    No. The tours are beginner friendly and guides give instruction at the launch.

    When can I see bioluminescence?

    Summer into early fall for the glow; comb jellies run later in the year.
