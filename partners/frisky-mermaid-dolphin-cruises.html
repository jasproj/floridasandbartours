The operation

Hoteliers who moved to the water

Deandra “Dee” and Steve Holcomb founded the company in 2018 after more than thirty years in luxury hospitality, both in executive roles at Hilton and Embassy Suites properties. Steve, a Certified Hotel Administrator, runs fleet operations, safety and maintenance; Dee handles guest experience and community relations and formerly chaired the Visit Pensacola board.

The captains are named on the site: Maryjo Loucks, who goes by Captain MJ, a Coast Guard 25-ton master with a background in education; Captain Steve Crowder, a 50-ton master the company credits with a 100 percent dolphin sighting record; and Captain Ally Laurent, a 50-ton master and, per the company, the first female captain at that level in the region. The fleet is all tri-toon, taking up to 49 passengers, with additional docks at Sabine Marina and behind Shaggy’s.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

715 Pensacola Beach Blvd, with additional docks at Sabine Marina and behind Shaggy’s. Check which your booking uses.

Licence

Pontoon rentals are bareboat; Florida requires a boater education card for anyone born after January 1, 1988.

Snorkel gear

Adult equipment is included on the snorkel stop, half-day and all-day trips.

Other services

The company also runs burial-at-sea charters, booked separately.
