The operation

Six guests, two boats, no crowded tour boat

The About page says the company was started to get away from crowded tour boats “where you can barely hear the captain or see the wildlife.” Captain Brian is listed as owner and captain; Josh is the other owner, running bookings and systems from shore. Captains Kris, Nate and Paul are named on the crew page. Every sail is skippered by a USCG-licensed captain.

Public sails take six guests per boat. Groups of seven to twenty go out as a bareboat charter on one or both catamarans. The site claims more than 4,000 five-star reviews across platforms; Google alone shows 3,966 at 5.0. Reviewers mention complimentary water and soda and that BYO champagne is fine.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

Cape Crossing Marina, 290 Marine Harbor Drive, Merritt Island. Parking is on site.

Cancellation

Full refund until five days before; inside five days only with Trip Protection (10 percent), which allows changes to the day before. Same-day changes are never refunded.

Weather

Go or no-go is decided close to departure; the operator cancels only for unsafe conditions and then refunds or reschedules.

Drinks

Water and soda aboard per reviewers; BYO is welcome.
