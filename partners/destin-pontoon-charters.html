The operation

Named boats, a captain on each, and a slide

The fleet reads like a jukebox: Osprey, Sweet Caroline, Takin’ It Easy, Chillin’ The Most, Nemo, Happy Hour, Island Time, Dancing Queen, Waterloo, Mongoose and It’s Tricky. Each is booked by name, which means you know what you are getting rather than drawing whatever is left at the dock.

Boats scale from six guests on a two-hour charter to thirty-two with a restroom aboard. Every charter is captained. What comes with it: paddleboards, pool floats, a lily pad, a large cooler with ice and bottled water, and a Bluetooth speaker, with snorkel gear added on the three-hour cruise. Crab Island is a shallow submerged sandbar just inside the East Pass, which is why half of Destin anchors there.

    The site does not list captains by name; reviewers mention Tanner, Joey and Nick.

    The one to book

    Private Pontoon with a Slide

    A double-decker for up to six, two hours, captained, with a slide off the top deck and the paddleboards and floats already aboard. For a family afternoon at Crab Island this is the whole thing in one booking.

    Two hours is $299. The flat pontoons for six start at $229.99; the bigger boats run to $1,700 for four hours and thirty-two guests.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Departure

    314 Harbor Blvd, Destin. A van and car shuttle to the boats can be added at booking.

    Included

    Captain, paddleboards, pool floats, lily pad, large cooler with ice and bottled water, Bluetooth speaker. Snorkel gear on the three-hour cruise.

    Crab Island

    A shallow submerged sandbar just inside the East Pass, not a true island. Water is waist deep in most of it.

    Restrooms

    On the larger boats only; check the listing before booking a long trip with children.
