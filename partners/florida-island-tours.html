The operation

Captain Nate, and a boat that drafts almost nothing

Captain Nate Lapierre grew up in New Hampshire and, by his own account, made a pact with his brother at twelve to come back to Florida and work on the water. He holds a US Coast Guard 100-ton master’s licence, certification as a Florida Master Naturalist and an Oregon Master Naturalist, CPR and first aid, and an FWC vessel livery operator credential.

The boat is a 26-foot Carolina Skiff 258 DLV with a 200-horsepower Yamaha, which he reckons he has run more than 600 days across Cape Romano, Marco Island, the Ten Thousand Islands and the Gulf. Six guests is the cap on every trip, public or private. The dock is at 220 Goodland Drive, across from Stan’s Idle Hour.

The one to book

Barrier Island Shelling Tour

Two and a half hours out to the islands only reachable by boat, with complimentary shell bags and an actual lesson in where to look and what you are holding. Junonias, lion’s paws and horse conchs all turn up on this stretch.

Adults are $129, children $99, and the same trip goes private for six at $625. The half-day Super Shelling run is $199.
