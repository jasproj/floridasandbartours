The operation

One boat called Salt Therapy

Captain Chris grew up on the Gulf Coast and spent the late seventies and eighties living on Eleuthera in the Bahamas. He is a US Coast Guard veteran and a USCG-licensed master captain, and the site lists a licensed electrician’s ticket and work as a chef alongside the boating, which is the sort of resume that explains why reviewers keep describing the trips as customised rather than scheduled.

The boat is Salt Therapy, a 21-foot NauticStar deck boat with a 150-horsepower Yamaha, Coast Guard rated for ten but run with a maximum of six. Aboard: a four-speaker stereo, a large cooler, full safety gear, Garmin GPS and VHF, a bimini for shade, boarding ladders fore and aft, a freshwater sink and cutting board, a power pole for anchoring in the shallows, and sun pads.
