The operation

A rental dock that briefs you properly

Speed Dock sits at 3637 Del Prado Boulevard South with direct access to the Caloosahatchee and, through it, Pine Island Sound, Sanibel and the Gulf. The fleet runs from a 19-foot Sea Ray SPX and a Bayliner VR6 at the entry end through Hurricane and Chaparral deck boats, Four Winns and Regal bowriders, Key West and Sea Fox center consoles, and Harris, Crest, Godfrey, Avalon and Regency pontoons.

What reviewers keep returning to is the handover. Boats are described as well maintained and clean, the checkout as efficient, and each one goes out with an iPad carrying navigation charts and specific routes, which matters on a coast where the wrong channel means a grounding. Rentals run by the day, with multi-day rates that drop the per-day cost sharply.

    The site does not list staff by name; reviewers mention Bastian and Captain Tom.

    The one to book

    Crest and Godfrey Pontoons

    For a sandbar day with a group, the 250-horsepower pontoons are the right call: shade, open seating, a swim ladder and enough power to get down the river without burning the afternoon.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Dock

    3637 Del Prado Blvd S, Cape Coral, with direct river access.

    Licence

    Florida requires a boater education card for anyone born after January 1, 1988. Sort it before you arrive.

    Navigation

    Each boat goes out with an iPad carrying charts and the routes the dock recommends.

    Multi-day

    Two- to seven-day rates cut the per-day cost; the largest boats are only offered multi-day.
