The operation

Owner-run since 2009

Captain Steve Jones took over the business in 2009. He is a Florida native and a USCG-licensed captain with more than thirty years on the water; the site also names Mike Rose, a long-serving member of staff, which in a nine-boat operation means you tend to deal with the same two people each time.

The fleet is deliberately simple: four NauticStar deck boats from twenty to twenty-two feet, four 22-foot pontoons with names rather than numbers, and a 26-foot Panga. Everything rents by the half or full day, with kayaks, paddleboards and jet skis alongside. A captained sunset cruise runs at $350 for people who would rather not drive.

Before you go

Know before you go

Dock

465 N Tamiami Trail, Unit 4, Venice, with intracoastal access and the Gulf through the Venice jetties.

Licence

Rentals are bareboat; Florida requires a boater education card for anyone born after January 1, 1988.

Shark teeth

Venice calls itself the shark tooth capital; the operator runs a shark tooth excursion alongside the rentals.

Captained option

A sunset cruise at $350 if nobody wants to drive.
