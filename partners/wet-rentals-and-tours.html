The operation

Six jet skis in 2018, nine locations now

The About page says the company launched in spring 2018 with six jet skis at a single location and has grown to nine: the Marriott Suites on Sand Key, the Sheraton Sand Key, the Holiday Inn Clearwater Beach, the Clearwater Beach Municipal Marina, the JW Marriott, the Chart House Suites, Bada Bing Water Sports in St. Petersburg and the Westin Tampa Bay.

The fleet it lists is 140 jet skis, 11 boats, 9 jet cars, tiki boats and a dolphin tour boat, plus banana boats, water trikes, kayaks and paddleboards. Rentals run freestyle, meaning you take the machine out yourself inside a marked area; guided tours put a lead rider in front of you for 90 minutes to two hours.

    The site does not list staff by name; reviewers mention Joey, Nick, PJ, John, Jen, Donovan, Mikey, Christian and Captain Morgan.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Locations

    Nine docks from Sand Key and Clearwater Beach to St. Petersburg and the Westin Tampa Bay. Confirm which one your booking uses.

    Licence

    Florida requires a boater education card for anyone born after January 1, 1988; the shops issue the temporary certificate on site.

    Freestyle vs guided

    Freestyle rentals stay inside a marked area; guided tours cover more water with a lead rider.

    Prices by site

    Jet ski rates differ between locations; the JW Marriott listings run higher than Sand Key.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Where do I go?

    One of nine docks; the booking names the location. Most are at resorts on Clearwater Beach.

    What does it cost?

    $120 an hour for a jet ski, $28.99 for a seat on the dolphin boat. Checked October 2, 2026.

    Do I need a boating licence?

    Anyone born after January 1, 1988 needs a Florida boater education card; the shop handles the temporary one.

    Can I bring drinks?

    On the tiki boat, yes. The sightseeing boat has no bar or restroom.
