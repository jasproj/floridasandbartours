The operation

A couple’s plan from 2012, on the water since 2015

The About page says Chris Reilly and his wife Sole began researching a Marco Island watersports company in 2012 while both worked in local hospitality, and opened in 2015. Chris holds a master captain’s license and a master diver certificate; he spent childhood summers fishing the Marco backwaters with his grandfather. Sole, an industrial designer, shaped the brand.

The site says captains are employed rather than subcontracted and are drug and alcohol tested. Tours leave Goodland Boat Park at 750 Palm Point Drive; the sunset eco tour leaves Caxambas Marina on the south end of Marco. Reviewers name Captains Lloyd, Elliot and Colleen.

The one to book

Marco Island Dolphin Tour

Two hours into the Ten Thousand Islands to find the resident bottlenose pods, with a money-back guarantee if none appear. Manatees and shorebirds are common on the same run. Up to 14 guests on the shared boat.

Kids 3 to 10 are $65, toddlers $19. The private version is $475 for up to six.

Before you go

Know before you go

Departure

Goodland Boat Park, 750 Palm Point Drive, Goodland, about ten minutes from Marco. The sunset tour leaves Caxambas Marina.

Cancellation

Full refund if cancelled 48 hours before, per the homepage.

Group size

Up to 14 on shared dolphin tours, 17 on the sunset boat; private boats for 6 or 12.

Rentals

Kayaks and paddleboards from $40 for two hours, with delivery mentioned by reviewers.
