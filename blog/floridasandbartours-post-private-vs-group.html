Private vs Group Sandbar Tours Florida: Which Should You Book?

Florida's Gulf coast has a broad range of both shared and private sandbar tours at prices that make the math interesting. Here's the actual breakdown with real prices from Gulf coast operators.

Short answer: For solo travelers and couples, shared tours ($40-$79/person) win. For groups of 4+, private charters often match or beat shared per-person costs while giving you full flexibility. A Gulf coast deck boat at $325 for 4 hours split 6 ways is $54/person - less than every shared tour listed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a private sandbar charter worth it in Florida? +
For groups of 4+, yes. A Gulf coast deck boat at $325 split 6 ways is $54/person - below most shared tour prices with full BYOB and flexibility.
How much does a private sandbar charter cost in Florida? +
Gulf coast private rentals with captain: $325-$600 for 4 hours. Key West private charters: $525-$799. Destin: $400-$650 for 4 hours.
What is the cheapest sandbar tour in Florida? +
Jupiter Family Sandbar Cruise at $40/person. Shell Key Sunset Cruise at $50/person. Crab Island Tiki Boat at $79/person.
